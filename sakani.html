03

The Problem

Finding suitable student accommodation can be challenging, especially for students relocating to a new city. Students need more than just a room; they need a comfortable living environment that matches their budget, preferences, and lifestyle.

04

The Solution

Sakani provides a student-focused housing experience that combines accommodation discovery with AI-assisted roommate matching, helping students explore suitable living options and find compatible roommates.
